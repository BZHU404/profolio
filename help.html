How I can help

A website isn't just about who I am — it's about what we could do together. If any of these sounds useful, come say hi.

01 Fix your keyboard (and other broken gadgets) with me

Got a keyboard, mouse, or pair of headphones you're about to throw away? Bring it over. We'll open it together, figure out what's wrong, and try to fix it — or at least understand it. I can also show you how to swap switches, repaint a case, or mod the look.

Who it's for
Anyone with a device they hate to toss.
Time
One or two hours, on a weekend.
How to start
Catch me at school with the thing in your bag, or drop by my workshop.

My own Cherry Mx9.0 went through this — new switches, a fresh paint job, and a daughterboard swap. It's on the Project page.

02 Build your own simple website, start to finish

Want a homepage of your own but don't know where to begin? I'll walk you through exactly how I made this one — design it in Figma first, then write it in plain HTML/CSS. No coding experience needed. We can have your first version up in a couple of afternoons.

Who it's for
Classmates who want their own page, starting from zero.
Time
One to two hours a weekend, for two or three weekends.
How to start
Tell me what you'd want on your page, and we'll start with a rough sketch.

Not sure which one fits? Just find me — the worst that happens is we talk over coffee.
